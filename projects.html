CUBE SAT

TCube Satellite for Atmospheric Data Collection
The project involves designing, building, and deploying a Cube Satellite (CubeSat) engineered to gather vital data on atmospheric conditions within the troposphere, the lowest layer of Earth's atmosphere. The CubeSat will leverage advanced sensors and communication technologies to perform the following key functions:
Monitoring Atmospheric Conditions:
Equipped with high-precision sensors, the CubeSat will measure parameters such as temperature, pressure, humidity, and wind patterns. This data will provide insights into weather dynamics, cloud formation processes, and climate variability.
Detecting Heat Signatures:
The satellite will use thermal imaging and infrared sensors to identify and analyze heat signatures across the troposphere. This capability is crucial for studying phenomena such as urban heat islands, wildfires, and variations in Earth's surface temperature distribution.
Tracking Carbon Accumulation:
Advanced spectrometers onboard will measure concentrations of carbon dioxide (CO₂) and other greenhouse gases. This data will help track carbon emissions, identify sources of pollution, and assess the effectiveness of carbon mitigation strategies.
Real-Time Data Transmission:
The CubeSat will be equipped with a robust communication module to transmit collected data to ground stations in real-time. This ensures the timely availability of critical information for analysis.
Applications in Research and Policy:
Environmental Research: Provides detailed datasets for scientists studying climate change, weather forecasting, and ecological impact assessments. Policy Making: Offers actionable insights for policymakers to implement effective strategies for reducing greenhouse gas emissions and adapting to climate change. Disaster Management: Assists in early detection of events like wildfires and severe weather conditions, enabling quicker response times.
Technological Highlights:
Compact Design: The CubeSat's modular structure ensures cost-effective deployment via piggyback launches on larger rockets. Energy Efficiency: Solar panels will power the satellite, ensuring long-term operation with minimal energy requirements. Durability: Built with materials designed to withstand the harsh conditions of the troposphere, including high turbulence and temperature variations.

Spending Insight Pro Model 💵📊🔍📈

Monthly expenditure analysis using Data Science.
This project uses numpy, pandas, seaborn, and matplotlib libraries to build a model that analyzes spending categories, amounts, balances, and purchase dates. The data, collected from bank statements, is visualized using PowerBI.

Steps performed to implement the model:
1. Collected data on expenditures, such as amount spent, category, and date.
2. Uploaded the data into a Python environment using the Pandas library.
3. Performed exploratory data analysis to understand the data and its distribution.
4. Visualized the data to identify patterns and relationships.
5. Performed feature engineering to extract relevant information.
6. Pre-processed the data by dropping irrelevant columns and encoding categorical variables.
7. Split the data into training and testing sets.
8. Built and trained a regression model using the Random Forest algorithm.
9. Evaluated model performance using metrics such as Root Mean Squared Error.
10. Used the model to predict expenditure amounts for new data points.
